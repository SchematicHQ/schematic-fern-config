Schematic is a usage-based billing platform for AI and software companies. Meter consumption, bill on credits, enforce every limit in real time, and give customers a live view of what they are spending.

Usage-based pricing keeps gaining ground because it lines up what a customer pays with what they actually get out of the product. It stalls when buyers cannot predict the bill. They worry about runaway spend, and they worry about being throttled with no warning. Schematic gives your customers visibility into what they are using, forecasts of where they will land, and spending limits they set themselves, so a usage-based deal clears review at any size of company.

Not everything should be metered. Seats, flat-rate tiers, and hybrid plans that pair a platform fee with metered overage all run on the same catalog, the same real-time entitlement checks, and the same customer-facing controls. You can price each part of your product the way it actually sells, and change the mix later without touching application code.
