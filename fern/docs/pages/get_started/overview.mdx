Schematic sits between your application and your billing system. You define plans, features, and limits in the dashboard, your code asks whether a company is entitled to something and reports what it used, and Schematic keeps access, invoices, and the customer's billing UI in agreement. Repricing and repackaging are configuration changes rather than deploys.

## Where to start by role

Schematic is one product that several teams operate together, so a page often belongs to more than one of these lists. Pick the role closest to the work in front of you and follow the links from there.

- **[Build with Schematic](/developers)** — the engineering hub, covering the SDKs, the REST API, sample apps, keys, and environments.
- **[Flags](/feature-management/flags)** — how an entitlement check resolves at runtime, and what your code gets back.
- **[Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks)** — subscribe to plan changes and entitlement triggers to drive your own workflows.
- **[Stripe integration guide](/integrations/stripe-integration-guide)** — wire Schematic and Stripe together so subscriptions and entitlements stay in step.
