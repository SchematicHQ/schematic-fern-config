Schematic is a billing engine with a deep Stripe integration. It runs the billing work Stripe Billing does not do well: metered AI and token consumption, credits, real-time usage enforcement through entitlement checks, and the negotiated deals that break self-serve billing tools. Usage-based pricing is what brings most teams here, and seat-based and hybrid models are supported just as fully.

Usage-based pricing stalls in procurement when buyers fear runaway bills and throttling without warning, so Schematic gives your customers visibility into their consumption and control over their spend, and both sides can sign knowing usage will stay inside budget.
